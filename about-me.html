A little more about me

Hi, I’m Dareen.

I’m a UX researcher and CX strategist who brings customer perspectives into product and service decisions.

Most recent experience

My most recent role is as a Customer Experience Specialist at Canlan Sports, a recreational sports company operating multi-sport complexes across Canada and the United States. I own CX research, Voice of Customer (VoC) reporting, and insights across 17 program and brand lines, including adult and youth hockey leagues, camps, instructional programs, tournaments, and retail operations.

How I approach research

I started as a freelance UX researcher, working across industries. My research experience in Canada, the United States, and Indonesia has deepened my understanding of and respect for people with different backgrounds, cultures, and characteristics—an outlook I bring to customers and teammates alike.

Grounded in psychology, I listen closely, ask what’s behind people’s experiences, and work with teams to make sense of what we learn and decide where to focus next. I’m always eager to keep learning.
